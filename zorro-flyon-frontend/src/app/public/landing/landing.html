Questions people ask first

What if I break something?
Tell the owner right away. Most repairs are a small fix or a shared cost, and the return log keeps a record for both sides.
Does it cost anything?
Joining and borrowing are free. Owners can ask for a small deposit on expensive items.
Who can see my address?
Only people you accept a request from. Everyone else sees your street name, not your door.
